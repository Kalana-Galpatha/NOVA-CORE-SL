# Astronaut Health Monitoring: synthetic dataset

Generated by `scripts/generate-dataset.mjs` from the Crew Vitals Console simulator (seed 42, 4 simulated days per environment).

**All data is simulated. It is not real astronaut data. Limits are illustrative, not medical requirements.**

## telemetry.csv
One row per crew member every 5 minutes across 4 environments (spacecraft, station, rover, base) and 4 crew. Random scenarios (tachycardia, fever, arrhythmia, sensor dropout, CO2 scrubber fault, cabin leak, solar particle event) start and end during the run.
- Vitals: heart rate, systolic BP, SpO2, core temperature, respiration, HRV (blank when the sensor is lost).
- Cabin: CO2, pressure, ppO2, radiation dose rate. Crew: sleep, exercise minutes, cumulative dose.
- Scenario flags (`fx_*`, `env_*`) are the ground truth for what was injected.
- `*_level` columns: 0 nominal, 1 watch, 2 caution, 3 warning. `risk_level` / `risk_label` is the worst of them.
- `active_alerts`: titles of alerts the app's alert engine had raised for that crew member or the cabin.
Good for: risk classification, anomaly detection, scenario detection.

## chatbot_qa.jsonl
1080 chat-format examples (system with live telemetry, user question, assistant answer) for fine-tuning a small LLM with LoRA. Answers come from the app's onboard engine, so a model trained on this learns the app's telemetry-grounded, decision-support style. Retain the "decision support, not diagnosis" rules.
